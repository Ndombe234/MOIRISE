# M14 — COLLECTION & REWARDS

## Goal
Cards/collectibles, achievements, cosmetics, titles and reward inventory.

## Integrity
Inventory and reward grants are deterministic and server-authorized. AI can recommend or design candidates but cannot mint arbitrary production rewards.

## UI
Collection is a contextual section under Profile/System rather than a permanent global button.

## Acceptance
Grant, consume, equip, unequip, duplicate prevention, history and recovery all work.
